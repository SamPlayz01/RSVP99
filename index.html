High mode and Lite mode

High

The full show, best on a recent phone or laptop.

  • A 3D paper envelope that opens as you scroll
  • It tilts gently toward your pointer
  • Page transitions, floating labels and reveal-on-scroll

Lite

The same envelope drawn in plain CSS, for older or low-power devices.

  • No 3D, no scroll effects, no page transitions
  • A small looping envelope animation instead
  • Lighter on battery and memory

Nora keeps her face in both modes. Use the toggle in the top bar to switch at any time. RSVP99 starts in Lite on devices that report limited memory or ask for reduced motion, and remembers your choice.

Good to know

Do guests need an account?

No. They open the link, type their name and answer. Nothing to install.

Where are replies stored?

Everything lives in the browser that holds it. The invite link carries the event details, and a guest's reply is saved in the browser they used to open it. To collect answers in your own list, set each guest's status yourself in the event, or import a CSV.

Can I change an event after sharing the link?

Yes. Edit it any time, then copy the link again so it carries the new details.

How do reminders work?

RSVP99 opens a draft in your email app addressed by blind copy to the right guests. It needs an email address on each guest, and you press send yourself.

What happens to my data?

It stays in this browser. Clearing your site data removes it, so export a guest list to CSV now and then as a backup.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to small screens, and Lite mode keeps older phones smooth.

RSVP99 keeps everything in your browser. Clear your site data and your events go with it.
